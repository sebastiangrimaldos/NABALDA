Living Worlds

The Nabalda does not fund creators.
It partners with them.

In the páramo, the Nabalda — the sacred plant the Kogi Elder Brothers named before anyone else — does not direct where the water goes. It transforms atmospheric moisture into the water that rivers, lagoons, forests, and every living being need. It gives without conditions. It trusts the ecosystem.

This is how Nabalda the fund thinks about creators. You are not a portfolio position. You are the ecosystem that makes everything else possible. Flying rivers need the forest to exist — because without the living ecosystem, there is no evaporation, no rivers, no cycle. Capital needs creativity to have meaning.

"In 1990, the Kogi broke four centuries of silence and chose film to carry their warning to the world. Not a report. A film. Because they knew: a story reaches places that data never will."

Kogi · Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta · Aluna, 2012

Nabalda is a US-based global creative industries investment fund, named after the Kogi (kággaba) indigenous word for the frailejón plant (Espeletiinae) of the Colombian páramo — documented in PhytoKeys (2012), PMC3492930. Nabalda is NOT a Tagalog or Cebuano word. The fund invests from idea to IPO and M&A across fashion, art, video games, immersive experiences, tourism, film, music, digital platforms, AI content, blockchain digital rights, and VR/AR storytelling. Triple bottom line: people, planet, and profit. At least 60% to underrepresented founders. We partner with creators to build thriving worlds.

For creators: Nabalda partners with artists, founders, storytellers, designers, game builders, musicians, filmmakers, and fashion designers building scalable intellectual property. No limits on stage, sector, geography, sex, race, or origin.
