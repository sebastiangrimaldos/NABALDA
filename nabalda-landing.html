About

Guardians

Nabalda is an impact investment fund dedicated to empowering creative industries. We believe creators are architects of culture and innovation. Like the Nabalda plants that nurture vital ecosystems by transforming mist into water, we provide the capital and strategic support needed for creators to build thriving worlds — worlds of art, technology, and social impact.

The name "Nabalda" comes from the Kogi people's word for the frailejón — a plant of the high páramo that captures the mist and releases it gradually, feeding rivers and life below. We are that alchemy: patient, deliberate, transformative.

Emparamarse

To honor a territory. To adore a lagoon. To greet a forest of mosses, lichens, rocks, fungi, grasslands, shrubs, frailejones, streams, and pastures. Between the sky and the earth.

Investment Thesis

The next cycle of global growth will be driven not by software as a tool, but by content and intellectual property as strategic assets.

01

Defendable IP

We seek assets that can be licensed, expanded, and protected globally — where value resides in rights and brand, not just code.

02

Transmedia Scale

The potential for a project to leap from a niche (a game, a song, a story) to global markets: merchandise, film, fashion, licensing.

03

Intergenerational Value

We prioritize business models that generate returns across cycles — financially sustainable, culturally enduring, socially impactful.

04

Underrepresented Builders

At least 60% of investments allocated to founders from underrepresented backgrounds. Diversity is not a filter — it is our thesis.

Where We Invest

Media Tech

New forms of content distribution and consumption. Platforms that redefine how stories reach audiences at scale.

IP-Centric Startups

Companies creating franchises from scratch — animation, comics, transmedia narratives, cultural universes built to last generations.

Creative AI

Tools that augment — not replace — the capacity to produce high-quality IP. AI as the new creative infrastructure.

Gaming & Worlds

Infrastructure for persistent creative worlds. Games as cultural platforms, not just entertainment products.

Creator Economies

Cultural Preservation Tech
