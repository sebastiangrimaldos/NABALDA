Flying Rivers

Capital that moves
like water.

In the páramo, flying rivers are atmospheric currents that carry moisture across entire continents — from the Amazon rainforest to the high peaks of the Andes — where the Nabalda captures them and transforms them into the water that feeds everything below.

Capital partners in Nabalda are flying rivers. They bring liquidity. Nabalda holds it, transforms it, and releases it gradually into creative ecosystems where it becomes something new — intellectual property, cultural legacies, thriving worlds.

"The flying rivers need the forest to exist. Without the ecosystem below, there is no evaporation. Without evaporation, no rivers. Without rivers, no forests. Capital and creativity are the same cycle."

The Opportunity

The most underfunded sector
in the global economy.

Creative industries represent one of the fastest-growing sectors in the world — yet they remain systematically underserved by institutional capital. The gap between the cultural and economic value creators generate and the capital they receive is one of the great inefficiencies of our time.

Nabalda deploys patient, strategic capital into companies building scalable intellectual property with the potential to become global franchises and cultural legacies that outlast us.
